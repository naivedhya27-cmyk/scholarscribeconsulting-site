What you receive

An evidence pack with one page per issue: the report’s words beside the source’s, with links to both documents.

Every footnote goes through five checks

  1. The cited work exists as cited: authors, title, year, journal or publisher, volume, pages and link.
  2. Read in full, the work says what the report says it says, at the page cited.
  3. Every figure is recalculated from the cited data.
  4. Each conclusion follows from its evidence and agrees with the rest of the report.
  5. Where a report has been corrected, every change between versions is checked.

The rules for what counts as an issue are set before checking begins. Your reports and our findings stay between us.

Evidence synthesis · subcontracted capacity

Systematic review capacity for teams carrying more evidence work than hours.

Scholar Scribe Consulting runs systematic reviews, literature searches and trial‑trustworthiness screening as subcontracted capacity for health economics, medical communications and clinical evaluation teams. Every review is run to PRISMA 2020 and signed by the physician who assessed it.

What we deliver

Systematic reviews and meta-analysis

Protocol and PROSPERO registration, search strategies across MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL and the trial registries, dual independent screening with recorded adjudication, extraction to your template, risk of bias by RoB 2 or ROBINS‑I, meta-analysis in R, and GRADE certainty tables.

Targeted and rapid literature reviews

Scoped reviews for reimbursement and HTA dossiers, including NICE, PBAC, CADTH and the EU Joint Clinical Assessment. You receive de-duplicated record counts, the PRISMA flow diagram, and a search log that holds up inside a submission.

Trial trustworthiness screening

INSPECT‑SR, the 21-check tool Cochrane endorsed on 24 September 2026, applied to the trials in your review before they reach your analysis. Detailed below.

Clinical evaluation literature reviews

Literature appraisal for clinical evaluation reports and post-market clinical follow-up under EU MDR 2017/745, with the appraisal criteria stated and the reasoning for every excluded record written out rather than summarised.

Trustworthiness screening, in detail

A review is only as sound as the trials inside it. INSPECT‑SR asks 21 questions about a randomised trial across four domains before that trial is allowed to carry weight in an analysis.

Done by hand the tool needs two assessors working independently, with a senior adjudication wherever they disagree. We hold a local copy of the full Retraction Watch database, 73,356 records, so the retraction and post-publication notice checks return immediately; the remaining checks are worked by hand against the published guidance, which is the only way they can be worked. You receive the completed checklist for every trial and the decision log behind it.

INSPECT‑SR is published under CC BY 4.0 and licensed for commercial use with citation. We cite it in every deliverable.

How the work is run

AI assists the first pass. A physician signs every decision.
Screening and extraction are accelerated by machine, then reviewed by a clinician who is accountable for the call. No decision reaches you unread.
You get the decision log, not just the result.
Every include, exclude and unresolved call is recorded with its reason, so your team can audit the review instead of taking it on trust.
Searches are reproducible as run.
Strategy, databases, dates and hit counts are supplied exactly as executed, never reconstructed after the fact to look tidier.
Nothing is reported that was not read.
A record we could not retrieve stays marked as not retrieved. A check we could not complete is reported as incomplete, with the reason.
