When to use it

Before release, on receipt, and after a correction

For firms that write reports

Before release

Each reference, quote, figure and conclusion is checked before the report reaches your client. Every issue comes back with its evidence and the correct citation.

For agencies that commission reports

On receipt

The same check, before a report is accepted, paid for or published.

For reports that have been corrected

After a correction

Each correction is checked, and the whole report is checked again for anything new that went wrong.
