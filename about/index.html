Systematic reviews, targeted literature reviews and trial trustworthiness screening for health economics, medical communications and clinical evaluation teams.

How we work

A physician signs off every finding

AI reads each cited source in full and speeds up screening and extraction. Every finding and every decision is then checked and signed off by a physician.

Rules set in advance

What counts as an issue, and what gets a study included or excluded, is written down before the work begins.

Everything is logged

Every issue links to its sources, and every review comes with its decision log and search log.

Confidential

Reports and documents you send, and the findings we return, are kept confidential.
