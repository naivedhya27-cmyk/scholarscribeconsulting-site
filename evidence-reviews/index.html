Evidence reviews

Systematic reviews for evidence teams

We run systematic reviews, literature searches and trial trustworthiness screening as subcontracted capacity for health economics, medical communications and clinical evaluation teams. Every review is run to PRISMA 2020 and signed by the physician who assessed it.

What we deliver

Four kinds of review

Each is quoted against an agreed protocol and delivered to UK, EU, US and Australian deadlines.

01

Systematic reviews and meta-analysis

Protocol and PROSPERO registration, search strategies across MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL and the trial registries, dual independent screening with recorded adjudication, extraction to your template, risk of bias by RoB 2 or ROBINS-I, meta-analysis in R, and GRADE certainty tables.

02

Targeted and rapid literature reviews

Scoped reviews for reimbursement and HTA dossiers, including NICE, PBAC, CADTH and the EU Joint Clinical Assessment. You receive de-duplicated record counts, the PRISMA flow diagram and a search log suitable for a submission.

03

Trial trustworthiness screening

INSPECT-SR, the 21-check tool Cochrane endorsed on 24 September 2026, applied to the trials in your review before they reach your analysis.

04

Clinical evaluation literature reviews

Literature appraisal for clinical evaluation reports and post-market clinical follow-up under EU MDR 2017/745, with the appraisal criteria stated and the reasoning for every excluded record written out in full.

Trustworthiness screening

INSPECT-SR, in detail

INSPECT-SR asks 21 questions about a randomised trial, across four domains, before that trial is allowed to carry weight in an analysis.

21

checks, across four domains

21-45

minutes per trial, assessed by hand

29%

of 206 COVID-19 trials excluded for integrity concerns

60%

fall in usable trials per meta-analysis once screening is applied

Figures from the published evaluation of the tool across 23 reviews. A further 38% of those trials could not be resolved either way.

Done by hand, the tool needs two assessors working independently and a senior adjudicator wherever they disagree. We hold a local copy of the full Retraction Watch database, 73,356 records, so the retraction and post-publication notice checks return immediately. The remaining checks are worked by hand against the published guidance. You receive the completed checklist for every trial and the decision log behind it.

INSPECT-SR is published under CC BY 4.0 and licensed for commercial use with citation. We cite it in every deliverable.

Method

How the work is run

A physician signs every decision

Screening and extraction are accelerated by machine and then reviewed by a clinician, who is accountable for each decision.

A decision log with every review

Each include, exclude and unresolved decision is recorded with its reason, so your team can audit the review.

Searches reported as run

Strategies, databases, dates and hit counts are supplied exactly as executed.

Gaps are reported

A record we could not retrieve is marked as not retrieved. A check we could not complete is reported as incomplete, with the reason.
